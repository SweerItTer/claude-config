# Output Patterns

## Standard research dossier

Use this structure for substantial research:

```text
# <Work / Topic> Research Dossier

## 1. Scope
- research questions
- version / adaptation / spoiler scope

## 2. Source map
- primary / creator-proximate / secondary / community coverage
- important evidence gaps

## 3. Findings
### RQ1 ...
- finding
- evidence
- confidence / limits

## 4. Analytical synthesis
- character / relationship / plot / quest / worldbuilding lenses as relevant

## 5. Conflicts and uncertainties

## 6. Reusable design patterns

## 7. What should not be generalized

## 8. Source index

## 9. Open research questions
```

## Character study

Prefer this structure when the task asks why a character works:

1. Initial audience model
2. Contradictions and withheld information
3. Desire, fear, values, and agency
4. Relationship mirrors and pressure points
5. Key reversals/recontextualizations
6. Player/viewer participation in the bond
7. Creator commentary
8. Audience interpretations
9. Reusable construction patterns
10. Limits and counterexamples

## Plot / quest study

1. Active dramatic questions
2. Information-release timeline
3. Escalation and reversals
4. Choice architecture
5. Consequence timing
6. Quiet phases and contrast
7. Payoff and aftermath
8. Production/design constraints
9. Transferable patterns

## Comparative study

Create the comparison dimensions first, for example:

| Dimension | Work A | Work B | Evidence quality | Mechanism-level difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Useful dimensions include introduction, information release, relationship progression, agency, consequence, world-system visibility, environmental storytelling, and thematic reinforcement.

## Reusable pattern card

```text
Pattern: Delayed sympathetic recontextualization
Mechanism: Establish a strong negative model, then reveal costly evidence that makes the earlier behavior legible without erasing its harm.
Evidence basis: <claim IDs / citations>
Effect: Forces active model revision; can create attachment without requiring moral absolution.
Conditions: Earlier behavior must remain coherent after the reveal.
Failure mode: Late backstory functions as an excuse rather than recontextualization.
Transfer rule: Reuse the information-order mechanism, not the original backstory, dialogue, or sequence of scenes.
```

## Writing rules

- Put evidence next to the claim it supports.
- State uncertainty briefly and explicitly.
- Prefer mechanism-level language over praise such as “well written.”
- Do not use “the creator wanted...” unless creator evidence exists.
- Do not pad the report with plot recap that does not answer a research question.
